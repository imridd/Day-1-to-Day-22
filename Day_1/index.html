Cat origin

Domestic cats (Felis catus) originate from the African wildcat (Felis silvestris lybica), first domesticated in the Near East about 9,000–10,000 years ago. Scientific and archaeological evidence shows that all modern domestic cats descend from a single wild ancestor: the African wildcat. These wildcats lived across North Africa and the Middle East and gradually adapted to human settlements during the rise of early agriculture
